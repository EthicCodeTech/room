Room, by EthicCode

Install
1. Drag Room into the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications.

If macOS says it cannot verify Room
This build is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm once.
1. Open Room once and close the warning.
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security.
3. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Room, then confirm.
You only do this the first time.

Good to know
- Room needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
- Room reads your folders to measure them. Everything stays on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded.
- macOS may ask to let Room look inside Desktop, Documents, Downloads or other apps' data. Choose Allow to include them. Anything you decline shows as private.
- Room never deletes anything without showing you the exact amount first. Items that might be yours go to an undo vault for 7 days.
